# Space Marine chapter audit — 16 October 2026

All six requested chapters have substantive local-versus-website differences. I read **161 distinct datasheets**, covering **635 roster entries** across the six factions, including **64 chapter-specific sheets** and each expanded Legends section. The six audited bundles match the installed copies in **G:\AAA** byte for byte. This is an audit; no rules data or installation was changed.

The website is the comparison reference requested by the user. Directions below are **local → website**. The complete register contains every compared sheet, source links, field differences, rule-name/prose flags, attachment changes, price/size checks and local entries absent from the source roster. Shortened prose, catalogue metadata and equipment bonuses are distinguished from confirmed mechanical differences.

## Black Templars

Most chapter-specific base statlines already agree. The remaining differences include actual rules and weapon changes, rather than a wholesale toughness mismatch.

- [Crusader Squad](https://www.40k.app/factions/black-templars/units/crusader-squad): Righteous Zeal surge distance is D6+2 locally versus D6+1 on the site. The site offers 10/20-model tiers at 160/305 points. Requesting 20 through our unit-size helper produced 12 models, so the size/composition handling needs investigation before treating this as a point-price disagreement.
- [Sword Brethren Squad](https://www.40k.app/factions/black-templars/units/sword-brethren-squad): Thunder Hammer damage is 2 locally versus 3 on the site.
- [Castellan](https://www.40k.app/factions/black-templars/units/castellan): heavy bolt pistol and chainsword Strength are 4 versus 5. Its support targets gain Company Heroes and Bladeguard Veterans on the site.
- [Gladiator Valiant](https://www.40k.app/factions/black-templars/units/gladiator-valiant): 165 → 145 points before later-copy surcharges. [Repulsor Executioner](https://www.40k.app/factions/black-templars/units/repulsor-executioner): 275 → 265. Their multi-meltas also differ: S9/AP-4/D6/Melta 2 locally versus S10/AP-3/D3+2/Melta 3.
- [High Marshal Helbrecht](https://www.40k.app/factions/black-templars/units/high-marshal-helbrecht) has additional source attachment targets and a printed Transhuman Strategist label not found on his local sheet.
- Grimaldus's three relic effects are **already present locally inside Temple Relics**; they are not three missing abilities. His supercharged plasma keyword list does differ.

## Dark Angels

This is a substantial chapter-specific refresh, including several older Terminator and mounted statlines.

- [Lion El'Jonson](https://www.40k.app/factions/dark-angels/units/lion-eljonson): T9/W10 → T10/W16. His pistol modes gain range and Strength; sweep attacks change from 16 to 12 and gain Cleave 2. The site also has revised doctrine, aura and protection abilities.
- [Belial](https://www.40k.app/factions/dark-angels/units/belial), Deathwing Knights and Deathwing Terminators are T5 locally versus T6 on the site. Sammael, Ravenwing Black Knights and the Ravenwing Command Squad also show T5 → T6.
- [Azrael](https://www.40k.app/factions/dark-angels/units/azrael): Masterful Tactician changes from a Command-phase CP gain to granting a doctrine to a visible friendly unit within 9 inches in the Movement phase. The Watcher protection is presented separately.
- [Ezekiel](https://www.40k.app/factions/dark-angels/units/ezekiel): W4 → W5, changed weapons and a level-three psychic ability package absent from the local sheet.
- [Ravenwing Command Squad](https://www.40k.app/factions/dark-angels/units/ravenwing-command-squad): Narthecium changes from returning a destroyed model to healing D3+1 wounds. Heroic Intervention wording changes from 0CP to a 1CP reduction.
- [Land Speeder Vengeance](https://www.40k.app/factions/dark-angels/units/land-speeder-vengeance): T8/W10 → T9/W11, changed plasma battery profiles and a source Deep Strike label. Darkshroud has additional source defensive/mobility labels, including a printed 5+ invulnerable save.

## Ultramarines

- [Kaius Konorius](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/kaius-konorius) is **missing as a local datasheet**. His name and 100-point price already exist in our MFM input, but no selectable unit package exists. The site presents him as a Support character with Veteran Bodyguard and Calgar's Champion.
- [Roboute Guilliman](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/roboute-guilliman): the Emperor's Sword is A14/S8 locally versus A10/S10 on the site. His other compared fields are much closer than several older characters.
- [Chief Librarian Tigurius](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/chief-librarian-tigurius): W4 → W5, Storm of the Emperor's Wrath attacks D6 → D3+6, changed rod profile and new psychic-roll abilities.
- [Captain Titus](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/captain-titus): revised Sustained Hits conditions, a new once-per-battle/army Strength-and-healing ability, and different fight-on-death handling. The local ability can leave him alive after fighting; the site's version removes him after resolution.
- [Cato Sicarius](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/cato-sicarius): changed sword modes, revised reactive movement limit and a Leader-to-Support interaction when Calgar is in the army.
- [Marneus Calgar](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/marneus-calgar) matches the older local title “Marneus Calgar in Armour of Antilochus.” The site has changed gauntlet modes, a doctrine-granting Master Tactician and Thunderhawk Insertion.
- [Victrix Honour Guard](https://www.40k.app/factions/ultramarines/units/victrix-honour-guard): weapon buffs, revised banner/objective rules and a source defensive wound modifier. Wardens also have changed weapons, model/save presentation and banner wording.

## Space Wolves

- [Bjorn](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/bjorn-the-fell-handed): T9 → T10. Ancient Tactician changes from gaining CP to reducing a stratagem's cost. Several guns also differ.
- [Arjac](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/arjac-rockfist) and Logan are T5 locally versus T6 on the site. Wolf Guard Terminators have the same T5 → T6 discrepancy. Shield wounds need an equipment-aware comparison, not a blanket W4 → W3 change.
- [Wolf Priest](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/wolf-priest): Healing Balms changes from returning a model to healing D3+1 wounds.
- [Iron Priest](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/iron-priest): vehicle support moves from the Command phase to the start/end of his Movement-phase move, with different targeting and attack benefits.
- [Grey Hunters](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/grey-hunters): Cunning Hunters changes from wound rerolls to melee Strength/AP bonuses near an objective. Old Greymanes permits splitting the unit before battle.
- [Njal](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/njal-stormcaller): revised lightning profiles and a new psychic-roll package. Ragnar's Blood Claws benefit changes from wound rerolls to +1 Strength and Sustained Hits 1.
- [Wolf Scouts](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/wolf-scouts): M7 → M8, changed weapons and Haywire Mine timing/range. Wulfen and the shield/hammer variant also have weapon and duration changes.
- [Headtakers](https://www.40k.app/factions/space-wolves/units/wolf-guard-headtakers) require special identity/composition handling: there are two local packages with the same name and mixed source price tiers. Their price flags are **not confirmed point errors**.

## Raven Guard

Both chapter characters have meaningful changes.

- [Aethon Shaan](https://www.40k.app/factions/raven-guard/units/aethon-shaan): Master of Shadows selects a friendly Infantry unit for charge rerolls on the site, rather than designating an enemy charge target. Blackwing Mantle also changes its covered stratagems and usage wording. His claws' Sustained Hits 2 become restricted against non-Monster/Vehicle targets.
- [Kayvaan Shrike](https://www.40k.app/factions/raven-guard/units/kayvaan-shrike): Blackout A2 → A1. Trifold Path changes from a 12-inch targeting restriction while leading to Stealth and reduced detection range. His reserve-return timing is worded at the end of the opponent's Fight phase on the site.

## Salamanders

Both chapter characters have meaningful changes.

- [Adrax Agatone](https://www.40k.app/factions/salamanders/units/adrax-agatone): the enemy OC effect changes from halving OC to -1 OC. Melee wound rerolls become reroll-ones normally and full rerolls under Assault Doctrine. Drakkis gains Strength and Malleus Noctum gains Devastating Wounds.
- [Vulkan He'stan](https://www.40k.app/factions/salamanders/units/vulkan-hestan): Forgefather changes from wound rerolls to +2 Strength for qualifying Melta/Torrent attacks against the nominated target. His spear is S6 → S7. The objective-based OC/Leadership/FNP package is substantially equivalent prose.

## Shared Marine findings

The six chapters still use their previous shared Marine copies; the recent website overlay was scoped to Blood Angels. The same shared discrepancies therefore recur across their lists.

Examples include Phobos Captain T5 → T4, Phobos Librarian and some Incursor/Reiver rows M6 → M8, Biologis OC3 → OC1, and Scouts T5 → T4. Numerous weapon profiles and keywords differ, along with generic Librarian powers, attachment eligibility and older Legends profiles. The register lists these separately for each faction.

Assault Terminators price at **195/390 locally versus 170/340 on the site**, before later-copy surcharges. Their shield W4 locally versus source base W3 plus a shield's +1 W is an **equivalent equipment representation**, not a confirmed wound nerf. Tarantula Sentry Battery has source two/three-model price tiers, but our size helper returns one model. Sicaran is one combined source sheet versus several local variants.

Most other evaluated prices agree. These are targeted exceptions, not evidence that every faction's point values are wrong.

## Source oddities and unresolved comparisons

- Headtakers has two different six-model prices, 115 and 170, while the composition text lists Hunting Wolves alongside Headtakers. A correct comparison needs the model mix; do not use total model count alone.
- Tigurius's source keyword list omits Imperium. Titus's Press the Attack contains broken inline markup around a conditional keyword.
- Azrael's separately printed Watcher rule does not state an expiry in its captured text. Do not silently insert a phase duration.
- The previously noted shared Apothecary ranged/Melee-range anomaly and conditional invulnerable saves recur on these same shared pages.
- Wardens' mixed human/Marine profiles, optional shields and separately embedded save rules need equipment-aware review before interpreting every stat-row flag as a mechanical discrepancy.
- Weapon-name flags are not automatic missing weapons. The comparison resolves articles/plurals and known aliases such as Raven's Talons, the Emperor's Sword, Corvus Hammer(s), and consolidated Sicaran; further mode/rename cases remain labelled for review.
- Units absent from the site's roster have not been inferred retired or removed. Every loadout permutation and army/detachment rule was not audited.
